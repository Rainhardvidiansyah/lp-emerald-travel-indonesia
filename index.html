Travel Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent details on island logistics, safety protocols, visa formalities, and how we handle changes on the ground.

How does your team handle unexpected tropical weather or ferry cancellations?

Because our team is based directly in Bali, Jakarta, and Labuan Bajo, we monitor harbor master advisories and domestic flight bulletins in real time. If sea conditions delay a ferry crossing to Nusa Penida or Komodo, our coordinators immediately adjust hotel check-ins, re-route land transfers, or arrange vetted flight alternatives at zero additional management charge.

What is included with your private vehicle and driver services?

Every private vehicle charter includes an experienced, licensed English-speaking local driver, modern air-conditioned vehicle, fuel, parking, and highway tolls. Your schedule remains completely flexible on the day: if you wish to stop at a roadside coffee plantation, linger longer at a waterfall, or alter a dinner route, your driver accommodates your rhythm.

How do payments and booking deposits work for custom itineraries?

We provide a transparent itemized invoice showing exact villa, flight, transport, and national park entry fees in your preferred currency (USD, EUR, AUD, or IDR). An initial deposit secures your villa dates and domestic flight tickets, with the remaining balance due prior to arrival via secure bank transfer or major credit card. No hidden conversion surcharges.

Do you assist with Indonesian entry visas and customs requirements?

Yes. We provide step-by-step guidance for Indonesia’s official 30-day Electronic Visa on Arrival (e-VoA), the mandatory electronic customs declaration (e-CD), and the Bali regional tourism levy. If your journey extends beyond 30 days, our local team handles the official immigration extension paperwork so you avoid wasting vacation days at government offices.

How does on-trip communication work while we are traveling?

Prior to your departure, we connect you with a dedicated bilingual trip coordinator on WhatsApp. From tracking your inbound international flight and meeting you outside baggage claim to sharing daily driver contacts and restaurant reservations, your coordinator is on call throughout your journey to answer questions and solve requests within minutes.

Have a question about specific islands, dates, or travel styles?

Our planners in Bali and Jakarta are available to review your ideas and answer logistical questions directly.
