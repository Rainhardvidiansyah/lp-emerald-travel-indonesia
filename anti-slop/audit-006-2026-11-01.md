# Anti-Slop Audit Report #006
Date: 2026-11-01
Scope: Section Destinations (`#destinations`) in `index.html`

## Numbered Findings List

1. **[HIGH] R-38 & R-40 — Broken 404 Image Paths Without Dimensions**
   - Violated Rule: R-38 (Real Content or Honest Placeholder) & R-40 (Performance & Media Attributes)
   - Reason: Seluruh gambar destinasi (`img/dest-bali.webp`, `img/dest-jakarta.webp`, `img/dest-bandung.webp`, `img/dest-puncak.webp`) merujuk ke folder `img/` yang tidak pernah ada di dalam proyek, menyebabkan gambar pecah (HTTP 404). Tag gambar juga tidak memiliki atribut `width` dan `height`.

2. **[HIGH] R-26 & R-39 — False Affordance on Cards**
   - Violated Rule: R-26 (Interactive Elements) & R-39 (Accessible Semantics & Affordance)
   - Reason: Setiap kartu destinasi memiliki efek hover zoom (`group-hover:scale-105`) seolah-olah dapat diklik untuk melihat rute/paket destinasi tersebut, namun merupakan elemen `<article>` mati tanpa tautan aksi atau detail navigasi.

3. **[MEDIUM] R-14 — Cookie-Cutter Destination Cards**
   - Violated Rule: R-14 (Feature Cards)
   - Reason: 4 kartu disusun dalam grid simetris identik (`aspect-[3/4]`, garis horizontal emas 2px, heading, teks 1 baris) tanpa variasi visual, tag kategori wilayah, atau kedalaman konten.

4. **[MEDIUM] R-13 — Decorative Glow Orbs**
   - Violated Rule: R-13 (Glow)
   - Reason: Memasang dua bola gradien kabur raksasa (`blur-3xl bg-brand-600/30` dan `blur-3xl bg-gold-500/15`) sebagai pemanis latar belakang tanpa fungsi hierarki yang jelas.

5. **[LOW] R-05 & R-16 — AI Template Formula & Content Inconsistency**
   - Violated Rule: R-05 (Layout Structure) & R-16 (Copywriting & Alignment)
   - Reason: Komposisi mengikuti formula standar AI (bar pil emas tengah + judul terpusat + grid simetris 4 kartu + 1 tombol tengah). Teks pengantar menyebut *"Dragons"*, namun daftar destinasi memuat Puncak alih-alih Komodo yang merupakan habitat komodo dan telah disebut di seluruh section lain (Hero, Video, How It Works).

## Follow-up Resolution Report (2026-11-01)

All findings (1-5) have been resolved in [index.html](file:///Users/mac/Documents/emerald/index.html#L633-L768):

1. **[RESOLVED] R-38 & R-40 — Broken 404 Image Paths Without Dimensions**:
   - Removed all references to the non-existent `img/` folder.
   - Powered the visual centerpiece (Bali) using the verified local high-resolution asset `assets/images/bali-beach.webp` with explicit `width="5702"`, `height="3801"`, and `loading="lazy"`.
   - Structured secondary destinations using clean, legible, high-contrast editorial cards without phantom image dependencies.

2. **[RESOLVED] R-26 & R-39 — False Affordance on Cards**:
   - Replaced dead cards with explicit, fully accessible action links (`<a>`) with &ge;44px touch targets (`Inquire Bali Itinerary →`, `Explore Komodo Routes →`, `Explore Java Overland →`) linking directly to WhatsApp with destination-specific inquiry text.

3. **[RESOLVED] R-14 — Cookie-Cutter Destination Cards**:
   - Transformed the symmetrical 4-card grid into an asymmetric editorial layout:
     - Prominent featured card for Bali & Nusa Islands (covering 6 columns).
     - Stacked regional route cards for Komodo National Park & Flores and Java Calderas & Heritage (covering 6 columns).
     - Added concrete travel data (recommended duration, best travel months, and specific activities).

4. **[RESOLVED] R-13 — Decorative Glow Orbs**:
   - Completely removed the stacked `blur-3xl` glow orbs (`bg-brand-600/30` and `bg-gold-500/15`). The section relies on crisp typography, subtle borders, and structural whitespace.

5. **[RESOLVED] R-05 & R-16 — AI Template Formula & Content Inconsistency**:
   - Removed the generic pill bar and replaced random domestic destinations (like Puncak) with destinations that match the product narrative: Komodo (dragons & phinisi yachts) and Java (Bromo & Borobudur).
   - Added `#paket` anchor tag so header menu item "Packages" successfully scrolls to this section without ghost links (satisfying R-24).
