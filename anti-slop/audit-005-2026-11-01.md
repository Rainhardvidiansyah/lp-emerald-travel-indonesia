# Anti-Slop Audit Report #005
Date: 2026-11-01
Scope: Section Why Emerald (`#why-emerald`) in `index.html`

## Numbered Findings List

1. **[LOW] R-16 & C-5 — Generic AI Boilerplate Claims Without Concrete Evidence**
   - Violated Rule: R-16 (Copywriting & Buzzwords) & C-5 (Evidence Over Claims)
   - Reason: Kelima poin alasan ("Local Expertise", "Personalized Service", "Reliable & Professional", "Competitive Pricing", "24/7 Support") berupa klaim kosong template AI tanpa rincian operasional nyata tentang bagaimana Emerald Travel menjalankannya.

2. **[MEDIUM] R-04 & R-14 — Cookie-Cutter Icon List Structure**
   - Violated Rule: R-04 (Icons) & R-14 (Feature Cards / Lists)
   - Reason: Menggunakan deretan lingkaran ikon seragam (`h-12 w-12 rounded-full bg-brand-600`) dengan ikon tipis Lucide generik (pin, heart, checkmark, price tag, clock) tanpa diferensiasi brand.

3. **[LOW] R-05 & Part 1 — Decorative Left Stripe & Slogan Clichés**
   - Violated Rule: R-05 (Layout & Page Structure) & Part 1 (Decorative Elements)
   - Reason: Menggunakan dekorasi garis tepi kiri berwarna (*colored left stripe* `border-l-4 border-gold-500`) yang dipasangkan dengan slogan klise template AI *"Your Journey. Our Commitment."* serta pengulangan balok pil emas.

4. **[HIGH] R-26 & R-39 — Dead End Section Lacking Final Action / Next Step**
   - Violated Rule: R-26 (Interactive Elements) & R-39 (Interaction Affordances)
   - Reason: Sebagai konten penutup sebelum akhir halaman, section ini terputus begitu saja tanpa tombol aksi, kontak penutup, atau jalur konversi bagi pengunjung yang telah tervalidasi kepercayaannya.
