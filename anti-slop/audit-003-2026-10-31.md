# Anti-Slop Audit Report #003
Date: 2026-10-31
Scope: Section Video (`#video`) in `index.html`

## Numbered Findings List

1. **[LOW] R-05 — Monotonous 2-Column Section Rhythm & Alternating Background**
   - Violated Rule: R-05 (Layout & Page Structure)
   - Reason: Layout section video menduplikasi persis tata letak section sebelumnya (`#tentang`): split 2 kolom (teks di kiri dengan garis aksen emas di atas, media di kanan) dengan satu-satunya variasi hanya mengganti warna background dari putih ke krem.

2. **[LOW] R-15 & R-16 — Redundant CTA & Template Copywriting**
   - Violated Rule: R-15 (CTA) & R-16 (Copywriting)
   - Reason: Mengulang tombol CTA generik "Design My Dream Trip" yang identik dengan tombol di Hero, serta copy pengantar yang sangat singkat dan klise tanpa konteks apa yang sebenarnya ditampilkan di video.

3. **[MEDIUM] R-40 — Low-Resolution Stretched Thumbnail Without Dimensions**
   - Violated Rule: R-40 (Performance & Media Attributes)
   - Reason: Menggunakan thumbnail beresolusi rendah `hqdefault.jpg` (480x360 rasio 4:3) yang dipaksa stretch ke dalam container 16:9 (`aspect-video`) tanpa atribut `width` dan `height`.

4. **[HIGH] R-39 — Missing Interaction Context on Play Control**
   - Violated Rule: R-39 (Accessible Semantics & Interaction Affordances)
   - Reason: Tombol putar hanya berupa lingkaran kuning dengan ikon segitiga tanpa indikator durasi, label status interaktif, atau keterangan bahwa aksi akan memuat pemutar video eksternal.

## Follow-up Resolution Report (2026-10-31)

All findings (1-4) have been resolved in [index.html](file:///Users/mac/Documents/emerald/index.html#L170-L245):

1. **[RESOLVED] R-05 — Monotonous 2-Column Section Rhythm & Alternating Background**:
   - Replaced the repetitive 2-column layout (which cloned `#tentang`) with a wide, cinematic showcase format (`max-w-5xl`) centered on film immersion.

2. **[RESOLVED] R-15 & R-16 — Redundant CTA & Template Copywriting**:
   - Removed the duplicate "Design My Dream Trip" CTA button. Added authentic narrative introducing the film's scope across Mount Bromo, Komodo, and Bali, supported by a 3-chapter contextual content strip beneath the player.

3. **[RESOLVED] R-40 — Low-Resolution Stretched Thumbnail Without Dimensions**:
   - Upgraded to high-definition `maxresdefault.jpg` with graceful fallback to `hqdefault.jpg`, accompanied by explicit `width="1280"` and `height="720"` attributes and `object-cover`.

4. **[RESOLVED] R-39 — Missing Interaction Context on Play Control**:
   - Replaced the generic round yellow icon with a refined, tactile pill control ("Watch Film (3 Min)") with glassmorphic backdrop, accessible duration label, and high-visibility keyboard focus indicators (`focus:ring-4 focus:ring-gold-400`).
