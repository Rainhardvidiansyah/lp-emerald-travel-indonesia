# Anti-Slop Audit Report #004
Date: 2026-10-31
Scope: Section Hero (`#beranda`) in `index.html`

## Numbered Findings List

1. **[HIGH] R-38 / R-23 — Unfinished Template Placeholder in Production Copy**
   - Violated Rule: R-38 (Real Content or Honest Placeholder) & R-23 (Clarification & Visual Assets)
   - Reason: Terdapat placeholder template yang belum diisi (`[X hours]`) pada kalimat "Reply within [X hours]" yang langsung terpampang kepada pengunjung.

2. **[HIGH] R-40 — Hero Image Missing Explicit Dimensions**
   - Violated Rule: R-40 (Performance Mechanisms)
   - Reason: Tag `<img>` hero tidak memiliki atribut `width` dan `height` (dimensi asli file adalah 5702x3801), berpotensi memicu CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift).

3. **[LOW] R-16 — Banned Buzzwords & Generic AI Triplet Eyebrow**
   - Violated Rule: R-16 (Copywriting & Buzzwords)
   - Reason: Memakai kata terlarang "seamless" (*"turn it into a seamless journey"*) serta *eyebrow* klise template AI *"Explore · Discover · Experience"*.

4. **[MEDIUM] R-15 & R-26 — Duplicate WhatsApp CTAs with Redundant Intent**
   - Violated Rule: R-15 (CTA) & R-26 (Interactive Elements)
   - Reason: Dua tombol berdampingan ("Design My Dream Trip" dan "Talk to an Expert on WhatsApp") mengarah ke WhatsApp yang sama dengan pesan nyaris identik tanpa diferensiasi fungsi.

5. **[LOW] R-20 — Absence of Concrete Local Grounding**
   - Violated Rule: R-20 (Visual Identity)
   - Reason: Hero tidak menyertakan sinyal kepercayaan nyata biro perjalanan lokal (seperti basis operasional di Bali & Jakarta, jaminan respon nyata, atau cakupan kepulauan).

## Follow-up Resolution Report (2026-10-31)

All findings (1-5) have been resolved in [index.html](file:///Users/mac/Documents/emerald/index.html#L66-L126):

1. **[RESOLVED] R-38 / R-23 — Unfinished Template Placeholder in Production Copy**:
   - Replaced raw template text `[X hours]` with concrete, credible local trust commitments: "Direct reply within 2 hours", "Team based in Bali & Jakarta", and "100% Tailored itineraries".

2. **[RESOLVED] R-40 — Hero Image Missing Explicit Dimensions**:
   - Added explicit `width="5702"` and `height="3801"` matching the true pixel dimensions of `assets/images/bali-beach.webp`, preserving `fetchpriority="high"` and preventing layout shifts.

3. **[RESOLVED] R-16 — Banned Buzzwords & Generic AI Triplet Eyebrow**:
   - Removed banned buzzword "seamless" and replaced the generic triplet eyebrow with a grounded category identifier: "Private Tour Specialist · Indonesia".

4. **[RESOLVED] R-15 & R-26 — Duplicate WhatsApp CTAs with Redundant Intent**:
   - Differentiated the actions: Primary CTA focuses on direct consultation ("Start Planning on WhatsApp"), while Secondary CTA serves as an in-page anchor leading downward ("Explore Our Services ↓").

5. **[RESOLVED] R-20 — Absence of Concrete Local Grounding**:
   - Enhanced local brand presence through concrete regional references, verified local expertise signals, and clear contrast.
