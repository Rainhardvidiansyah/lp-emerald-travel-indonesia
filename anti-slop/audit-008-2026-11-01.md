# Anti-Slop Audit Report #008
Date: 2026-11-01
Scope: Section FAQ (`#faq`) in `index.html`

## Numbered Findings List

1. **[HIGH] R-28 — Superficial Template Non-Answers Damaging Trust**
   - Violated Rule: R-28 (FAQ)
   - Reason: Jawaban FAQ berupa kalimat tunggal yang sangat malas dan minim informasi ("Our team is available 24/7.", "Yes, we guide you through the documents and requirements.") tanpa penjelasan operasional bagaimana layanan tersebut benar-benar bekerja.

2. **[LOW] R-05 & Part 1 — Repetitive AI Decorative Pill Bar**
   - Violated Rule: R-05 (Layout & Page Structure) & Part 1 (Decorative Elements)
   - Reason: Menggunakan balok pil emas terpusat (`<span class="block h-1 w-12 rounded-full bg-gold-500"></span>`) yang berulang di hampir setiap section.

3. **[HIGH] R-32 & R-39 — Missing Keyboard Focus States on Accordions**
   - Violated Rule: R-32 (Keyboard Accessibility) & R-39 (Accessible Semantics & Interaction Affordances)
   - Reason: Elemen `<summary>` tidak memiliki indikator fokus kustom (`focus-visible:ring-2`), menyulitkan navigasi pengguna keyboard saat menelusuri pertanyaan.

4. **[MEDIUM] R-26 — Dead End Section Without Escape Path for Unanswered Inquiries**
   - Violated Rule: R-26 (Interactive Elements)
   - Reason: Tidak menyediakan tautan bantuan langsung jika pertanyaan spesifik calon wisatawan (mengenai rute, visa, atau cuaca) belum terjawab di dalam daftar.
