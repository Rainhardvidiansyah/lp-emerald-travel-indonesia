# Anti-Slop Audit Report #001
Date: 2026-10-31
Scope: Section Services (`#layanan`) in `index.html`

## Numbered Findings List

1. **[HIGH] R-26 / R-39 — False Affordance & Non-Functional Interactive Styles**
   - Violated Rule: R-26 (Interactive Elements) & R-39 (Accessible Semantics & Interaction Affordances)
   - Reason: Setiap kartu fitur memiliki efek hover interaktif (`hover:-translate-y-1`, shadow, border, icon hover) seolah-olah dapat diklik, namun merupakan elemen statis `<article>` tanpa navigasi, link, atau aksi fungsional.

2. **[MEDIUM] R-14 — Copy-Paste Feature Cards Grid**
   - Violated Rule: R-14 (Feature Cards)
   - Reason: Enam kartu fitur dibuat dengan dimensi, padding, struktur layout, dan ukuran ikon yang seragam (3x2 grid) tanpa hierarki visual antara layanan utama dan pelengkap.

3. **[MEDIUM] R-04 — Generic AI Icon Presentation**
   - Violated Rule: R-04 (Icons)
   - Reason: Menggunakan set ikon stroke tipis generik gaya template AI yang dibungkus dalam container squircle identik (`h-14 w-14 rounded-xl bg-brand-50`) tanpa sentuhan identitas visual brand yang khas.

4. **[MEDIUM] R-13 — Decorative Glow Orb Background**
   - Violated Rule: R-13 (Glow)
   - Reason: Banner tagline di bagian bawah memuat elemen dekorasi bola gradien kabur (`blur-3xl bg-gold-500/20`) yang merupakan pola default visual AI tanpa fungsi hierarki yang jelas.

5. **[LOW] R-05 — Template Section Structure**
   - Violated Rule: R-05 (Layout & Page Structure)
   - Reason: Struktur section mengikuti tata letak klise template AI (garis aksen pill terpusat + heading terpusat 2 baris + grid simetris 6 kartu).

6. **[LOW] R-16 — Copywriting Repetition & Filler**
   - Violated Rule: R-16 (Copywriting & Buzzwords)
   - Reason: Penggunaan frasa template klise "Zero Hassle" pada heading utama terulang kembali pada deskripsi kartu tiket ("without the hassle") dengan deskripsi generik.

## Follow-up Resolution Report (2026-10-31)

All findings (1-6) have been resolved in [index.html](file:///Users/mac/Documents/emerald/index.html#L263-L370):

1. **[RESOLVED] R-26 / R-39 — False Affordance & Non-Functional Interactive Styles**:
   - Replaced static pseudo-interactive cards with fully functional, accessible interactive actions.
   - Core experience features a dedicated CTA button (`Plan a Custom Journey`) linking directly to WhatsApp consultation.
   - Each supporting logistical service card now includes an explicit, accessible text link (`<a>`) with a min-height touch target of 44px and contextual inquiry parameter for WhatsApp.

2. **[RESOLVED] R-14 — Copy-Paste Feature Cards Grid**:
   - Replaced the uniform 3x2 grid with an asymmetric, content-driven layout:
     - 1 prominent featured hero card (spanning 5 columns on desktop) emphasizing Emerald Travel's core value: Private Custom Tours & Itinerary Design.
     - 4 supporting pillars (spanning 7 columns on desktop in a 2x2 grid) for Stays, Transfers, Flights, and Visa assistance.

3. **[RESOLVED] R-04 — Generic AI Icon Presentation**:
   - Integrated contextual SVG iconography paired with meaningful status badges (`Core Experience`) and clean typography, establishing clear brand hierarchy.

4. **[RESOLVED] R-13 — Decorative Glow Orb Background**:
   - Removed the generic `blur-3xl bg-gold-500/20` AI glow orb from the section. Replaced the bottom container with a clean, authentic assurance banner.

5. **[RESOLVED] R-05 — Template Section Structure**:
   - Replaced the repetitive AI template structure with an editorial layout that prioritizes user guidance and real service differentiation.

6. **[RESOLVED] R-16 — Copywriting Repetition & Filler**:
   - Eliminated redundant clichés ("without the hassle" / "Zero Hassle"). Wrote clear, concrete copy detailing vetted boutique villas, licensed English-speaking local guides, and 24/7 on-ground assistance.
