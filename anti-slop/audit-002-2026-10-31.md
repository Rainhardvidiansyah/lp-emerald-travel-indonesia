# Anti-Slop Audit Report #002
Date: 2026-10-31
Scope: Section How It Works (`#how-it-works`) in `index.html`

## Numbered Findings List

1. **[LOW] R-05 — Cliché 3-Step Template Layout**
   - Violated Rule: R-05 (Layout & Page Structure)
   - Reason: Layout mengikuti formula AI yang sangat umum: judul terpusat "in 3 Simple Steps" + garis putus-putus penghubung (`border-dashed`) + 3 kartu identik dengan ikon lingkaran besar (`ring-8`).

2. **[MEDIUM] R-07 — Unnecessary Dot Grid Background Pattern**
   - Violated Rule: R-07 (Background)
   - Reason: Menggunakan pola titik-titik radial grid (`radial-gradient` 28px) sebagai latar belakang tanpa tujuan identitas brand.

3. **[MEDIUM] R-13 — Trend-Stacked Decorative Glow Orbs**
   - Violated Rule: R-13 (Glow & Trend Stacking)
   - Reason: Memasang dua bola gradien kabur raksasa (`blur-3xl` gold dan hijau) yang ditumpuk di atas background gradien tanpa fungsi hierarki visual.

4. **[LOW] R-16 — AI Marketing Cliché Copywriting**
   - Violated Rule: R-16 (Copywriting & Buzzwords)
   - Reason: Judul "Your Dream Trip in 3 Simple Steps" dan poin-poin langkah ("Tell us your dream", "Travel with confidence") terlalu generik dan tidak menjelaskan proses nyata perencanaan perjalanan.

5. **[HIGH] R-39 & R-26 — Lack of Clear Affordance & Next Action**
   - Violated Rule: R-39 & R-26 (Interactive Elements & Affordance)
   - Reason: Bagian How It Works tidak memiliki CTA fungsional untuk memulai proses setelah pengguna membaca langkah-langkahnya.

## Follow-up Resolution Report (2026-10-31)

All findings (1-5) have been resolved in [index.html](file:///Users/mac/Documents/emerald/index.html#L207-L300):

1. **[RESOLVED] R-05 — Cliché 3-Step Template Layout**:
   - Eliminated the formulaic 3-step grid, dashed line connector (`border-dashed`), and oversized icon rings (`ring-8`).
   - Implemented an editorial two-column milestone progression (`lg:grid-cols-12`) with an anchor context block on the left and structured chronological phases on the right.

2. **[RESOLVED] R-07 — Unnecessary Dot Grid Background Pattern**:
   - Removed the artificial `radial-gradient` dot background pattern. The section now uses a deep, solid brand emerald background (`bg-brand-900`) that creates natural visual contrast between the surrounding cream sections.

3. **[RESOLVED] R-13 — Trend-Stacked Decorative Glow Orbs**:
   - Completely stripped out the stacked `blur-3xl` glow orbs. The focus is restored entirely to legible typography, subtle borders (`border-brand-700`), and real content structure.

4. **[RESOLVED] R-16 — AI Marketing Cliché Copywriting**:
   - Replaced generic headings with concrete, benefit-focused narrative: named actual Indonesian destination regions (Bali, Komodo, Raja Ampat, Java), turnaround window (24–48 hours), itemized quotes, and real airport greeting procedures.

5. **[RESOLVED] R-39 & R-26 — Lack of Clear Affordance & Next Action**:
   - Added an explicit, accessible action anchor (`Chat With a Planner`) with minimum 44px tap target and a dedicated WhatsApp link (`https://wa.me/...`) so travelers can immediately act on the process.
